## At a glance

- **On-device first.** Opion processes photos, links and clipboard content on your device. We do not receive that content.
- **Advertising.** Opion is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may process your advertising ID, device and app information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data.
- **No account.** Opion does not require registration or login.
- **No sale.** We do not sell or rent your personal information.
- **Your choices.** Where the law requires it, you choose before personalized advertising is shown, and you can change your choice in the app.
- **Your rights.** You can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or an objection, wherever you live.

## Data at a glance

| Data | Collected by Opion | Shared with | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Advertising ID, device and app information, approximate location, ad interactions | Yes, by Google AdMob | Google (AdMob) | Advertising, measurement and fraud prevention |
| Photos, links, clipboard content or copied text | No | No | Not collected |
| Name, email address or phone number | Only if you email support | Email provider | Support |
| Precise location, contacts, SMS, health data, payment card | No | No | Not collected |

## Permissions

- **Photos:** the Android system photo picker is used to access only the image you choose. No broad storage access.
- **Clipboard:** read only to measure and overwrite it. The app never displays or transmits its content.
- **Internet:** used for advertising.
- **System Shortcuts:** opens Android settings panels.
- **Security Audit:** uses public APIs only; no root required.
